## Attack categories

### account_takeover
Credential theft or session hijacking used to seize control of a legitimate cardholder's account.
**Why it matters for this challenge:** GenAI amplifies this by making the fabricated narratives, documents, or identity signals far more convincing and cheaper to produce at scale, compared to manual fraud.

### synthetic_identity
A fabricated identity (real + fake data blended) used to open or operate a card account.
**Why it matters for this challenge:** GenAI amplifies this by making the fabricated narratives, documents, or identity signals far more convincing and cheaper to produce at scale, compared to manual fraud.

### refund_fraud
Fake or manipulated dispute/chargeback claims to extract refunds without legitimate cause.
**Why it matters for this challenge:** GenAI amplifies this by making the fabricated narratives, documents, or identity signals far more convincing and cheaper to produce at scale, compared to manual fraud.

### card_testing
Small, rapid transactions used to validate stolen card numbers before a larger fraudulent purchase.
**Why it matters for this challenge:** GenAI amplifies this by making the fabricated narratives, documents, or identity signals far more convincing and cheaper to produce at scale, compared to manual fraud.

### prompt_injection_scam
GenAI-era social engineering: attacker manipulates a bank chatbot, voice assistant, or customer via AI-generated scripts/prompt injection to authorize fraudulent actions. This is the hackathon's core 'novel emerging GenAI fraud' focus.
**Why it matters for this challenge:** This is the hackathon's core focus — GenAI tools let attackers generate convincing, personalized social-engineering scripts at a scale and quality that wasn't possible before, and can even attempt to manipulate AI-powered bank chatbots directly via prompt injection.

### deepfake_kyc
Deepfake audio/video or AI-generated documents used to bypass identity verification (KYC) checks.
**Why it matters for this challenge:** GenAI amplifies this by making the fabricated narratives, documents, or identity signals far more convincing and cheaper to produce at scale, compared to manual fraud.

## Overview
Person C implements the **Blue Team Fraud Detector** that evaluates payment transactions and contextual communication narratives generated by Person A (`seed_dataset.jsonl`) and Person B (`augmented_dataset.jsonl`).

### Features:
- **Multi-Key Rotation**: Rotates across 3 Gemini API keys (`GEMINI_API_KEY_1`, `GEMINI_API_KEY_2`, `GEMINI_API_KEY_3`) to balance API load.
- **Model Auto-Fallback Hierarchy**: `gemini-2.5-flash` â†’ `gemini-2.0-flash` â†’ `gemini-1.5-flash` on rate limits/quota hits.
- **Resume-Safe Incremental Output**: Writes each decision immediately to `detection_results.jsonl`, automatically resuming and skipping already-processed `case_id`s on restart.
- **Comprehensive Evaluation**: `evaluate.py` generates full accuracy, precision, recall, F1, confusion matrix, per-attack-type breakdown, and saves the formatted report to `evaluation_report.md`.
- **Closed-Loop Adversarial Feedback**: `find_weak_spots.py` exports false negatives into `weak_spots.json` for Person B to target. `compare_rounds.py` computes before/after recall lift.

| Script / Artifact | Description |
